Privacy Policy – Yorvixa AI Receptionist

Last updated: October 7, 2026

1. Introduction

Yorvixa AI Receptionist is an automated communication and appointment-assistance service designed for businesses such as clinics. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and shared when users communicate with the service through WhatsApp or related channels.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how a participating business configures the service, we may process your name, WhatsApp phone number, messages, appointment requests, preferred appointment times, service information, and other information that you voluntarily provide.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, provide automated customer support, schedule or manage appointments, send appointment-related messages, transfer conversations to authorized staff, and maintain service records for the participating business.

4. WhatsApp and Third-Party Services

The service may use WhatsApp and other technology providers for messaging, scheduling, hosting, databases, and AI-assisted automation. Their own privacy policies may also apply.

5. Data Sharing

Information may be shared with the business operating the service and with service providers necessary to operate the automation. We do not intend to sell personal information.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained as reasonably necessary to provide the requested service, maintain appointment records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security. Retention periods may vary by participating business.

7. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Choices and Data Requests

You may request information about personal data processed through the service or request correction or deletion where applicable. Requests should be directed to the business operating the service or to Yorvixa using the contact details below.

9. Children's Privacy

This service is not specifically directed toward children and does not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The updated version will be posted at the same public URL with a revised effective date.
